Godot has inbuilt functions that are marked with the prefix "_" 
for example _ready() is the engines on ready function
and thus cannot be renamed

godot also does not have public and private functions the prefix "_" is used within the code to signify that a function is private and not to be called outside the function

### Story of the game:
- The game is set in a post-apocalyptic world ravaged by the nuclear Armageddon caused by the great war. You the player are a "Cleanser" preservers of the powerful  bio-rejuvenation tanks able to cure any and all mental and physical ailments. having completed your initiation you have been charged with the management and construction of a brand new settlement, your task is to ensure the settlements survival by strategically managing its resources until the settlers arrive to live in the town. being a Cleanser you also bring along with you a single bio-rejuvenation tank, usable once a week you must manage its use wisely by strategically choosing each week who gets to use the tank, use it to safe the sickly child? or the farmer vital for food production, the choice is yours and the consequences may be fierce.

- The task is simple. Keep your town alive until more people arrive.
===============================================

### Rules: 
- At the end of each round, players must choose a town settler to heal.
- At the end of each round, the resources generated by the settlers alive will be tallied.
- at the end of each round, 1-3 events occur over the week which affect if settlers get sick and the chance to gain or lose resources
- The town must have enough food and water to feed each member of the town otherwise the town starves to death
- there are different sicknesses that settlers can catch, currently only radiation stages 1-3
- when a settler catches a sickness the have a chance to either survive it and be cured, get worse and progress to the next stage of severity or die with the more server stages of a sickness increasing likelihood of death
- Keep as many of the settlers alive for as long as possible.

### Core Gameplay Mechanic ###
the core Mechanic of the game is the players choice on who they grant access to the bio-rejuvenation tank each week.
use of the tank cures a settler or all mental and physical ailments that may be affecting them, keeping them a productive member of the town.

### supplementary Mechanics ###
-Resources
	to add another layer to the main mechanic, each settler generates or applies resources to the town, resources like food and water are vital to 	survival while Military and ingenuity affect how easy it is for the town to defend and survive. forcing the player to consider who they heal based 	on how much they contribute to the town
-Sickness System
	Settlers can get sick, settlers may survive it, get worse and progress in serverity, or Die unless cured, this is one reason why the player may  	select someone to heal them
-Events System
	each week after the player has made their choice a 1-3 events occur over the week these events can do anything from granting resources, taking 	resources or causing sickness and even death. the system is how we apply sickness and cause the problems the player must solve
-Mood System (not implemented)
	the other reason the player may have to heal someone. poor mood leads to less resource generation and even sabotage from those with poor mental 	health while good mental health can lead to increased resource generation and even better survival odds when sick
Trade System (not implemented)
	to add another layer of control each week traders may visit the town, allowing players the opportunity to buy and trade resources they may be 	desperate for

Current development progress:
Coding and development:
For the coding side of things, we are currently using Godot engine to develop our game. As our game is a resource management game, our core gameplay mechanic is relatively simple as the player only has to choose
which settler they would like to heal for the round. The player also has other button options that will give them different vital information that the player will need to know and keep track of in order to play the 
game. Currently, we have decided to do a very simple block-out of what we envision the game to feel like. We have a "Weekly Log" button that shows the events that have occurred in the week, a "Settler Log" button that 
shows the cards of the settlers currently in the town with their current status and resources they produce, and a "Progress Week" button that allows the player to proceed to the next week(round) of the game, when the round progresses each person sick has their sickness rolled on whether or not they survive, get worse or die, then resources generated by surviving town members are calculated with food and water being additive and military and ingenuity being added onto a base stat that only changes when permanent buffs are rewarded. then 1-3 events are rolled, whether or not the event pulled comes from the positive or negative list is based on (town ingenuity * 0.5)^2 the effects of the events are then applied and the game continues. As mentioned above the resources the player will have to manage are food, water, military and ingenuity, however we are looking towards adding a new resource called "scraps" that could be 
used by the player to make trades. We are also looking into more ways in which we could give the players more control over how they want to play the game, such as making the player choose which settler to starve if the settlement does not have enough food resource to feed all the settlers
and more interactive systems as well as systems that the player may need to worry about other then sickness and food. 
we currently also have a basic pause screen to allow the player to quit the game with plans to implement a settings menu there as well as on the main menu as well as save logic.
