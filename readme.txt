### Story of the game:
- The game is set in a post-apocalyptic world where the results of nuclear warfare still remain evident in even the air that the survivors breathe. Your role as a “Cleanser” is to lead an expedition team of settlers to test whether or not the new land you have found is suitable for the rest of the main group to move to. As the Cleanser, you must make choices that will prioritise the survival of the town by choosing the right settler to heal at the specific times. The settlers that you lead will generate the resources that the town will need to survive the period of time that the expedition team will need to hold out for until the main team arrives. There will be a random number of events that can happen every round which may help the town's survival or harm the town depending on the current stats of the town. 
- The task is simple. Keep your town alive to live another day. 
===============================================

### Rules: 
- At the end of each round, players must choose a town civilian to heal.
- At the end of each round, the resources generated by the civilians alive will be tallied.
- The town must have a specific number of resources in order to proceed to the next round, the specific number of resources required will be deducted from the number of resources the town currently has.
- There are different levels of sickness/ radiation that a civilian can incur. 
- The higher the level of the settlers sickness/ radiation, the higher the chances of the sickness/ radiation increasing which also leads to a higher chance of the individual dying. 
- Keep as many of the settlers alive for as long as possible.

Current development progress:
Coding and development:
For the coding side of things, we are currently using Godot engine to develop our game. As our game is a resource management game, our core gameplay mechanic is relatively simple as the player only has to choose
which settler they would like to heal for the round. The player also has other button options that will give them different vital information that the player will need to know and keep track of in order to play the 
game. Currently, we have decided to do a very simple block-out of what we envision the game to feel like. We have a "Weekly Log" button that shows the events that have occurred in the week, a "Settler Log" button that 
shows the cards of the settlers currently in the town with their current status and resources they produce, and a "Progress Week" button that allows the player to proceed to the next week(round) of the game. (Explain the 
way calculations are made here). As mentioned above the resources the player will have to manage are food, water, military and ingenuity, however we are looking towards adding a new resource called "scraps" that could be 
used by the player to make trades. We are also looking into more ways in which we could give the players more control over how they want to play the game, such as making the player choose which settler to starve as the camp
does not have enough food resource to feed all the settlers.
